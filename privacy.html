Privacy

What we keep

Nothing, unless you sign in. Without an account your records stay in this browser on your phone.

If you sign in: your email address (to send the sign-in code) and the records you save: farms, blocks, pickings, fermentation and drying runs, dry bean lots, chocolate batches and tastings. They are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database so they reach your other devices.

Trails

Trails, their stops and photos stay on your device only. They are never sent to us. Save a trail pack to keep a copy.

Lot passports

When you seal a passport, the records you choose are signed on your phone and published on the passport page. Location and notes are hidden unless you turn them on. Your email is never shown. Fingerprints of each day's entries are stamped into the public Bitcoin record through OpenTimestamps.

Visit counts

We count page opens per day and new devices. No cookies, no tracking services, no IP addresses kept. If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is counted.

Who else

Cloudflare hosts the app and the database. Resend sends the sign-in code and sees only your email. GitHub hosts the public code, not your data.

Your copy is your backup

We are one person. The cloud copy is for convenience, not a backup. Use Save all records on the Account page and keep the file yourself.

Delete

Write to info@koffykraft.coffee to delete your account and its records.
