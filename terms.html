What this is

Koko is a free tool made by one cocoa and coffee grower in Kerala. It is offered as it is, with no warranty. Cloud sync is a convenience, not a guarantee and not a backup.

Your records

You own what you enter. Keep your own copies (Account, Save all records; Trails, Back up all trails). We are not responsible for lost data from any cause.

Advice

Guides and numbers in the app name their sources and are guides, not guarantees. Your farm, your weather and your local extension officer come first. Read every label before using any chemical.

Limits

Up to 5,000 records per account. Do not upload other people's data, run bots against the service, or use it for spam.

If it closes

We will try to tell you, but cannot promise how much notice. Export your data when you see a notice.
